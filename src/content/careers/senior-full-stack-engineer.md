### Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced Senior Full-Stack Engineer to lead development on customer-facing and enterprise-scale platforms. You will collaborate directly with solution architects, product managers, and client stakeholders to convert complex business requirements into elegant, maintainable code.

### Responsibilities

- Architect and implement production features across React/Vue/TypeScript front-ends and Node.js/Go backend services.
- Define API contracts, data schemas, and microservice integration patterns.
- Drive engineering best practices through thoughtful code reviews, automated CI/CD workflows, and comprehensive test suites.
- Identify system bottlenecks and implement database and cache optimizations for high-throughput endpoints.
- Mentor junior and mid-level software engineers.

### Qualifications & Requirements

- 5+ years of production software engineering experience in modern JavaScript/TypeScript and cloud-native architectures.
- Deep proficiency with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and ORMs/query builders.
- Proven experience with Docker, containerization, and AWS/GCP cloud environments.
- Excellent written and verbal technical communication skills.
