### Role Overview

We are looking for a Cloud & DevOps Engineer to maintain and scale our deployment infrastructure. In this role, you will collaborate with cross-functional engineering pods to ensure that software moves from pull request to production with speed, security, and complete repeatability.

### Responsibilities

- Author and maintain Infrastructure-as-Code modules using Terraform and OpenTofu.
- Manage Kubernetes (EKS/GKE) clusters, ingress controllers, and service mesh implementations.
- Implement observability stacks including Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, and structured logging.
- Enforce infrastructure security baselines, vulnerability patching, and least-privilege IAM policies.
- Partner with development teams to eliminate pipeline bottlenecks and reduce deployment cycle times.

### Qualifications & Requirements

- 3+ years of DevOps/SRE experience running production workloads on AWS, GCP, or Azure.
- Strong proficiency with Linux system administration, bash, and container networking.
- Demonstrated experience configuring automated CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI).
