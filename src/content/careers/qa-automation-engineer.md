### Role Overview

We are looking for a skilled QA Automation Engineer who views quality as an engineering discipline. You will design, build, and maintain automated testing frameworks that protect our clients' mission-critical platforms against regressions while keeping deployment pipelines swift.

### Responsibilities

- Design robust automated UI, API, and regression test suites using Playwright, Cypress, and TypeScript.
- Integrate automated testing gates into GitHub Actions and GitLab CI/CD pipelines.
- Conduct load and stress testing using tools such as k6 or JMeter, isolating performance degradation points.
- Partner with product managers and engineers during sprint planning to formulate testable acceptance criteria.
- Maintain and expand cross-browser and mobile device verification test matrices.

### Qualifications & Requirements

- 3+ years of hands-on experience in automated software testing and framework development.
- Strong proficiency in TypeScript or Python for test automation scripting.
- Practical experience with REST and GraphQL API validation.
- Clear bug reporting, root-cause analysis, and defect lifecycle tracking.
