## From Industry Insight to Scalable SaaS

Converting specialized domain knowledge into a viable, subscription-based cloud product requires solving demanding engineering challenges: tenant data segregation, recurring billing lifecycles, self-service provisioning, granular permissions, and automated scaling.

Apt Infosoft provides end-to-end SaaS engineering for both venture-backed startups and established enterprises launching digital products. We lay the technical groundwork that lets you scale from early adopters to thousands of concurrent tenants without structural rewrites.

### SaaS Architecture Capabilities

- **Multi-Tenant Data Partitioning:** Database-per-tenant, schema-per-tenant, or partitioned shared-database architectures customized to your compliance and cost profile.
- **Subscription & Metered Billing:** Seamless integration with Stripe, Chargebee, and bespoke usage-metering pipelines for tiered, consumption-based, or seat-based monetization.
- **Identity & Access Management:** Self-service onboarding, Single Sign-On (SAML, OAuth2, OpenID Connect), MFA, and granular role/team hierarchies.
- **Tenant Management & Analytics:** Admin portals for provisioning, telemetry, feature flags, quota enforcement, and churn monitoring.

### Engineering for Low Unit Costs

SaaS unit economics depend heavily on infrastructure efficiency. We architect cloud-native solutions leveraging autoscaling compute, efficient caching, and serverless background workers to ensure infrastructure costs scale sub-linearly with customer revenue growth.
